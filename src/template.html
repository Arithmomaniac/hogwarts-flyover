About this flyover

A seven-minute journey through Hogwarts, its grounds, Hogsmeade and the Forbidden Forest, imagined within real Scottish mountain scenery.

The layout follows White Hound's The Map of Hogwarts, not the films' castle. This is one interpretation, not a definitive architectural plan or a discovered castle location.

The landscape combines real elevation and satellite data with modeled grounds, buildings and vegetation. The date controls the sunlight; the scene includes features from different books.
